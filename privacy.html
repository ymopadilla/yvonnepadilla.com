Privacy, in plain language

Last updated November 9, 2026

This page explains what information yvonnepadilla.com collects, why, and who helps me handle it.

What I collect

  • The contact form: your name, email, organization, the kind of help you're interested in, and your message.
  • Visit counts: anonymous numbers such as how many people visited and which pages they viewed. This site uses no cookies.

How I use it

I use what you send to reply to you and, if we work together, to do that work. I don't add you to a mailing list, and I never sell your information.

Services that help run this site

Each of these receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Formspree delivers the contact form to my email.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics provides the anonymous visit counts, without cookies.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering on this page. Your browser requests the fonts from Google, which means Google receives basic technical details such as your IP address.
  • My web host serves these pages and, like any host, keeps standard technical logs.

How long I keep it

I keep messages as long as I need them to work with you and keep normal business records. If you'd like me to delete what you've sent, just ask.
